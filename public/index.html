Optical Fault Quick Reference Cheat Sheet
Fault Type What You See OTDR Trace Cause & Corrective Action
Normal Link Normal optical power & clean eye 0.20 dB/km linear slope Link healthy. No action required.
Fiber Cut Total Loss of Signal (LOS) High reflection spike, drops to -50 dB Severed cable. Re-splice at reported distance mark.
Macro-Bend Excess attenuation Step drop with no reflection Bend tighter than 30 mm. Release pinch in tray/duct.
Dirty Connector Excess attenuation Step drop plus reflection spike Contaminated end-face. Clean with IPA wipe & re-seat.
Aged Fiber High loss across span Steep slope (> 0.25 dB/km) Water ingress / cable aging. Re-budget link or replace.
EDFA Failure Power & OSNR drop OTDR looks 100% normal Degraded 980 nm pump laser. Swap amplifier module.
Dispersion Eye squeezed shut (ISI) Power & OTDR are normal Pulse spread Δτ > UI. Add DCF or dispersion compensation.
Formulas: OSNR ≈ 58 + Pin − NF | Δτ = D · L · Δλ | BER = ½ erfc(Q/√2)
